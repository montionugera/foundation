- **A3** Names are dot-separated segments of letters, digits, `_`, and `-`.
  Case-sensitive; case-only collisions are rejected. `*` matches one segment, `**` any
  depth. The `@` prefix is reserved for Foundation. Hierarchy and struct fields share
  the dot. MQTT maps `.` to `/`. Letters are ASCII (decided by the person,
  2026-10-04). Widening to Unicode later stays backward compatible. `import` maps
  non-ASCII device tags to ASCII names. `types::Name::segment_char` gives the
  characters of a segment, so the rule has one place: the parse of a name and the
  defaults of `connector::import::names` call it. What an import puts in place of
  another character stays in `connector`. `laptop.architect`, 2026-10-16 14:34 UTC
  (https://github.com/synnaxlabs/foundation/issues/2868#issuecomment-6217809546).
